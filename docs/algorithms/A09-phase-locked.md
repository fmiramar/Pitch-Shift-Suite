# A09 — PhaseLockedShift

**Evidence:** published equations and local measurements. **Phase:** 4. **State:** standalone/SC functional checkpoint implemented; extended scientific/release gates open.

**Source:** Laroche & Dolson, 1999, *Improved Phase Vocoder Time-Scale Modification of Audio*, [primary paper, university-hosted copy](https://seniordesign.cs.tcu.edu/1516Ribbit/resources/ImprovedPhaseVocoder.pdf), §III-C equations (12)–(16), printed pp.329–330. Full file retrieved; these equations also inspected visually. No source-code license inferred.

**Equation trace:** [PhaseLockedShift.hpp](../../src/dsp/PhaseLockedShift.hpp), `updatePhases`: equations (5)/(6) propagate phase with `advance=Ωk H+principal(φa[k]−φa_previous[k]−Ωk H)` and `φs=φs_previous+r advance`. Identity locking uses (12): `φs[k]=φs[p]+φa[k]−φa[p]`. Scaled locking uses the previous region's peak in (15), unwraps adjacent analysis phases, then applies (16) with `β=(2+r)/3`. Four-neighbour peaks, midpoint regions and previous-region correspondence follow §III-C. The centered transform avoids alternating π offsets on bin migration. [Numerical tests](../../tests/phase_locked_smoke.cpp) check the equations, wrapped vertical phases, moving/split/merged peaks and unmatched initialization.

**API:** `PhaseLockedShift.ar(in, ratio=1, fftSize=2048, mode=1)`. Mono; scalar/control ratio in [0.5,2]. FFT size is initialization-only: 512/1024/2048/4096. Mode 0 is bare PV, 1 identity locking (default), 2 scaled locking. Hop is fixed at N/8. Independent language channel expansion does not provide stereo phase linking. The standalone constructor additionally takes sample rate and maximum block size (1–4096).

**Streaming realization (project-specific):** `renderFrame` analyzes a periodic Hann window centered at c=mH, circularly shifts its origin to FFT index zero, and waits for its final input sample. The unnormalized inverse FFT is divided by N. A synthesized frame is sampled at `u=rj`, windowed by `w(u)=0.5+0.5 cos(2πu/N)`, and added at output index `c+D+j`, where D=3N/2. Phase advance uses the virtual synthesis hop rH. At constant r, this realizes stretch then resample with fractional hops without rounding frame centers. Dynamic ratios use a 5 ms one-pole and the mean ratio over each analysis hop; this is a local approximation, not an exact time-warp or click-free guarantee.

**Normalization/resampling:** accumulate the actual squared Hann weights and divide by their sum. The denominator is at least 1.5, the minimum complete-coverage weight over the allowed ratio range, to prevent startup edge amplification. Unity uses exact integer frame reads; other ratios use a 33-tap Blackman-windowed sinc with interpolated fractional phases and conservative cutoff bands `0.475/(1+b/8)`. The sampled inverse-FFT frame is periodic. This finite interpolator approximates ideal resampling; it is not a general alias-free guarantee. The output ring has 8N slots: each frame contributes only ahead of the current audio sample, at most 2N samples into the future.

**Phase/endpoints:** bins below `max(N·1e-12, maximumMagnitude·1e-8)` initialize from analysis phase. Unmatched peaks do likewise. Silent frames clear peak ownership. Region ties choose the lower peak; a split may share a predecessor, and a distant new peak can be falsely associated with an old region. DC/Nyquist remain signed real coefficients and negative-frequency bins are conjugate mirrored. Unity DC/Nyquist reconstruct; nonunity endpoint behavior is limited by the real-bin policy and resampler cutoff. Precision/quality near DC or Nyquist is not established by the 440 Hz tests. There is no transient reset or formant preservation.

**Measured checkpoint:** A09 evidence (development record retained separately) includes Release/ASan/UBSan checks, 36 standalone tone cases, 576 impulse placements, 38 SC renders (36 new UGen, two stock controls), and live lifecycle/control checks. Unity error ≤4.01e-11; default unity delay 3072 samples, 64 ms at 48 kHz. Shifted impulse peaks vary with ratio/position. Locking improves amplitude stability on the selected chirp; this is not a subjective-quality result or a replication of the paper's consistency tables.
